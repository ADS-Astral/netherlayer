The bank, the circuit, the rails and the flight are solved as one system. The rails' inductance grows as the armature travels, which both pushes it and fights the supply, so the current is not something you set — it is what the circuit does.

Ideal in the ways that flatter a launcher: no friction or ablation in the bore, no rail erosion, a sabot that separates cleanly, and no allowance for the payload surviving what it is put through. A ballistic shot also cannot close its own orbit — reaching the altitude is not the same as staying there.
