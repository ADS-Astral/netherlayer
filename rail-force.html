Constant current gives constant force, so the acceleration is uniform and v = √(u² + 2as) holds exactly. A real pulse rises and falls, and the average force over the run is what matters — put that average in and the answer stands.

Bare electrodynamics: no friction, no air in the bore, no ablation, and no allowance for the armature’s own resistance. Every one of those takes velocity off the exit figure.
