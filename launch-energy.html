Drag is integrated step by step along a modelled ascent through the 1976 US Standard Atmosphere, so it responds to air density, vehicle size and how fast the vehicle is moving at each altitude — not a fixed allowance.

A preliminary-design estimate. Real vehicles differ with staging, throttling, trajectory shaping and engine performance that varies with altitude.

The bore is taken to be evacuated, so the projectile meets no air until the muzzle. Rail force follows F = ½·L′·I² at constant acceleration, with the inductance gradient L′ from the rail spacing and height.

A gun launch has to survive things this model does not compute: stagnation pressure and aeroheating at hypersonic speed in dense air, and the barrel acceleration shown. Drag here is the energy cost alone, not a survivability verdict.
